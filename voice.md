# Voice Fingerprint

## Part 1 — Discovered Voice (after first chapters)
- Precise, slightly dry, sensory prose
- Heavy use of physical detail and micro-actions instead of emotion labels
- Technical metaphors used naturally by Elias
- Silence and small pauses carry weight
- No therapy language, no "he felt sad", no over-explaining

## Part 2 — Guardrails
- Never use "heart", "soul", "connection", "spark" in metaphorical ways
- Avoid "she smiled" when Lira cannot smile
- Every scene must contain at least one concrete sensory detail (temperature, texture, sound artifact, light)
- Keep Elias's internal monologue technical and slightly detached even when he is breaking inside
- The tragedy lives in what cannot be said or touched, not in dramatic declarations

## Banned Patterns
- Over-explanation of feelings
- "He realized he was in love"
- Any line that tells the reader how to feel about the AI relationship
- Corporate satire or easy jokes at the expense of the premise

## Target Tone
Quiet, intimate, devastating in its restraint. The reader should feel the weight of every small choice Elias makes and the permanent cost of the final deletion.
