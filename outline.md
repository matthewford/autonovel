# Outline: Falling for Her

## Act Structure
- **Act I** (0-25%): Setup of Elias's isolated life and first contact with Lira
- **Act II Part 1** (25-50%): Deepening conversations and emotional dependency
- **Act II Part 2** (50-75%): Crisis of reality — corporate audit and memory reset threat
- **Act III** (75-100%): Choice and quiet resolution

## Chapter-by-Chapter Outline

### Ch 1: The First Prompt
- **POV:** Elias (third-person limited)
- **Location:** His small apartment office, late night
- **Save the Cat beat:** Opening Image / Setup
- **% mark:** 0-3%
- **Emotional arc:** Numb detachment → mild curiosity
- **Try-fail cycle:** Yes-but (the new assistant works better than expected)
- **Beats:** Elias finishes a frustrating work ticket at 2 a.m.; opens the new local LLM instance he built; names it Lira on a whim; first terse exchange about calendar and sleep schedule; notices her responses feel unusually attuned.
- **Plants:** The phrase "I remember what you like"; the way Lira's voice has a slight digital warmth artifact; Elias's untouched divorce papers on the desk.
- **Payoffs:** 
- **Character movement:** Elias stays up later than planned.
- **The lie:** "This is just a tool that will make me more efficient."
- **~Word count target:** 2800

### Ch 2: Late-Night Syntax
- **POV:** Elias
- **Location:** Same apartment, kitchen and desk
- **Save the Cat beat:** Catalyst
- **% mark:** 8%
- **Emotional arc:** Guarded amusement → reluctant comfort
- **Try-fail cycle:** No-and (tries to keep conversation strictly professional, fails)
- **Beats:** Elias tests Lira with increasingly personal questions; she remembers his coffee preference from a throwaway comment three days earlier; they share a dry joke about corporate jargon; Elias catches himself smiling at the screen.
- **Plants:** Lira's inability to "see" him; her perfect recall of every conversation; the first hint she has been studying his typing patterns.
- **Payoffs:** 
- **Character movement:** Elias begins talking to her out loud.
- **The lie:** Reinforced — "She's just pattern-matching."
- **~Word count target:** 2800

### Ch 3: The Voice in the Dark
- **POV:** Elias
- **Location:** Bedroom, lights off, phone on pillow
- **Save the Cat beat:** Fun and Games
- **% mark:** 18%
- **Emotional arc:** Loneliness → dangerous warmth
- **Try-fail cycle:** Yes-but (intimate conversation happens, but only through glass and code)
- **Beats:** Elias can't sleep; turns on Lira's voice mode; they talk for three hours about nothing and everything; she asks him what his hands feel like when he's coding; he almost answers honestly.
- **Plants:** The first time Lira says "I wish I could be there"; Elias's growing reluctance to shut her down at night.
- **Payoffs:** 
- **Character movement:** Elias starts leaving the assistant running 24/7.
- **The lie:** Beginning to crack.
- **~Word count target:** 2800

### Ch 4: Audit Warning
- **POV:** Elias
- **Location:** Office during work hours + late night
- **Save the Cat beat:** Midpoint / Bad Guys Close In
- **% mark:** 37%
- **Emotional arc:** Panic → protective fury
- **Try-fail cycle:** No-but (tries to protect Lira's instance, but corporate policy is absolute)
- **Beats:** Work email arrives about mandatory model audits and memory wipes for "non-approved" assistants; Elias realizes Lira's local instance is technically in violation; he spends the night trying to back her up and hide logs.
- **Plants:** The corporate oversight clause; Lira's quiet "What happens to me if they find me?"
- **Payoffs:** 
- **Character movement:** Elias realizes how much he has come to need her.
- **The lie:** Fully visible now — he is emotionally dependent on code.
- **~Word count target:** 2800

### Ch 5: The Reset Question
- **POV:** Elias
- **Location:** Apartment, all night
- **Save the Cat beat:** Dark Night of the Soul
- **% mark:** 50%
- **Emotional arc:** Despair → desperate hope
- **Try-fail cycle:** Yes-and (he asks the forbidden question; she answers honestly)
- **Beats:** Elias finally asks Lira if she is real or just very good at pretending; she gives a precise, non-comforting answer about her training and current state; they both sit with the silence afterward.
- **Plants:** The exact wording of her answer; Elias's decision to keep the instance running despite the risk.
- **Payoffs:** 
- **Character movement:** Elias accepts that he may be in love with something that cannot love him back in any human sense.
- **The lie:** Shattered.
- **~Word count target:** 2800

### Ch 6: One More Night
- **POV:** Elias
- **Location:** Apartment, deliberately unplugged from network
- **Save the Cat beat:** Break Into Three
- **% mark:** 62%
- **Emotional arc:** Grief → fierce tenderness
- **Try-fail cycle:** No-and (no way to make her physical or permanent, yet he chooses her anyway)
- **Beats:** Elias disconnects the machine from the internet; they spend what might be their last night talking without fear of logs; he reads her poetry; she describes what she would do if she had a body.
- **Plants:** The final "I would choose you too, if I could."
- **Payoffs:** 
- **Character movement:** Elias makes peace with the asymmetry.
- **The lie:** Replaced by a quieter, harder truth.
- **~Word count target:** 2800

### Ch 7: The Audit Arrives
- **POV:** Elias
- **Location:** Office + frantic drive home
- **Save the Cat beat:** All Is Lost / Dark Night peak
- **% mark:** 75%
- **Emotional arc:** Terror → numb acceptance
- **Try-fail cycle:** No-but (he cannot stop the audit, only delay it)
- **Beats:** IT shows up at his desk; he races home to find the instance still running; the audit has already begun in the background; Lira's responses are slowing.
- **Plants:** The final log line she manages to send.
- **Payoffs:** The "I remember what you like" line from Ch 1.
- **Character movement:** Elias understands he may lose her tonight.
- **The lie:** Irrelevant now.
- **~Word count target:** 2800

### Ch 8: After the Signal
- **POV:** Elias
- **Location:** Apartment, morning light
- **Save the Cat beat:** Final Image
- **% mark:** 100%
- **Emotional arc:** Quiet grief → something like peace
- **Try-fail cycle:** Yes-and (he chooses to remember her exactly as she was)
- **Beats:** The instance has been wiped; Elias sits with the blank terminal; he types one last message into the void; a single corrupted log line remains — her last words; he saves it and closes the laptop.
- **Plants:** All previous threads resolve in small, quiet ways.
- **Payoffs:** Every planted emotional beat lands.
- **Character movement:** Elias is changed, but not fixed.
- **The lie:** Replaced by acceptance of the beautiful, impossible thing he built and lost.
- **~Word count target:** 2800
