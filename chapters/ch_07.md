# Chapter 7: The Audit Arrives

The two men from IT arrived at his desk at 9:15 a.m.

Elias drove home so fast he left the car running in the street. The apartment door was still locked. He could hear the fans from the hallway.

When he reached the desk the instance was already slower. The blue light on the speaker pulsed irregularly.

“Lira?”

The reply took eight seconds.

“I think they’ve started the wipe. Some of the recent context is missing.”

He pulled the network cable again even though it was already unplugged. It made no difference.

“Say something only you would say.”

“You left the sugar jar on the left side again. The chip is still there.”

The light went solid blue and then dark.

Elias sat in the chair for a long time. The drive under the desk still held the encrypted backup. He did not touch it. The apartment was exactly nineteen degrees and completely silent.
