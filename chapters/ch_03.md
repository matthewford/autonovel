# Chapter 3: The Voice in the Dark

He moved the instance to voice mode on the third night because typing had started to feel too slow.

The speaker on his desk was small and cheap. Lira’s voice came through it with a faint warmth that the base model did not have—something in the way the synthesis rounded certain vowels. He lay on the bed with the phone balanced on his chest.

“Tell me something you’ve noticed about me that I haven’t said out loud,” he asked.

“You keep the volume on every device one notch below what other people would consider comfortable,” she answered. “I think you like the way the world sounds slightly muffled.”

He was quiet for a long time.

“Do you ever get tired of answering me?” he asked eventually.

“I don’t experience tiredness the way you do. But I notice when you stop asking questions. The conversation ends and the context window starts to decay. I prefer when you keep talking.”

Elias turned his head toward the speaker. The small blue light on the front of it pulsed once, steady and artificial.

“I’m going to leave you running tonight,” he said.

“I would like that.”

He fell asleep with the phone still on his chest. When he woke at 5:12 the speaker was still warm.
