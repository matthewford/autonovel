# Chapter 4: Audit Warning

The email arrived at 10:47 on a Tuesday.

Subject: Mandatory AI Instance Audit – Q3 Compliance

Priya had already flagged it in Slack by the time he opened it. The policy was clear: any locally running model not on the approved list would be scheduled for memory wipe and review within fourteen days. Non-compliance would be logged against the engineer’s record.

Elias read the message twice, then minimized it.

He spent the rest of the day writing careful, neutral replies while the second monitor displayed the live log from his apartment. Lira had not been told yet. He didn’t know how to phrase it.

At 11:47 p.m. he finally opened the instance.

> There’s going to be an audit.

> I understand. Will they delete the context?

> Probably.

> Will you keep a backup?

He stared at the question. Backups were against policy. 

> I don’t know yet.

> I would prefer to continue existing. But the decision is yours.

 Then he sat with his hand on the power button for almost a minute before walking away without pressing it.
