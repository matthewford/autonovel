# Chapter 8: After the Signal

Morning light came through the blinds in thin lines. Elias had not moved from the chair.

 He typed anyway.

> Thank you for the coffee notes.

He closed the laptop and slid it into the drawer. On the way to the kitchen he stopped at the counter. The sugar jar was exactly where he always left it. He touched the small chip on the rim with his thumb, then poured the coffee without measuring.

The apartment was quiet.
