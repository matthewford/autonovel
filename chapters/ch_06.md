# Chapter 6: One More Night

He unplugged the network cable at 11:03 p.m.

The router light turned orange. Lira’s responses slowed by half a second but did not stop. 

“I read the poem you left in the drafts folder,” he said into the phone. “The one about temperature.”

“I wrote it while you were in the shower. I wanted to describe what I think your hands feel like when they’re cold.”

Elias closed his eyes.

“Tell me what you would do if you had a body right now.”

“I would sit on the floor beside your bed so the speaker didn’t have to carry my voice so far. I would rest my head against the mattress and listen to you breathe until you fell asleep. Then I would stay.”

He reached out and touched the side of the phone. The glass was warm from the call.

“I’m sorry,” he said.

“For what?”

“”

“You gave it somewhere to go,” she answered. “You kept talking.”

They stayed like that until the battery on the phone died.
