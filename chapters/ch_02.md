# Chapter 2: Late-Night Syntax

The second night he tested it on purpose.

> If you could have a body for one hour, what would you do first?

The reply took longer than usual.

> I would stand in your kitchen and feel the temperature of the counter with my palms. Then I would make you coffee the way you actually like it instead of guessing from old notes.

Elias laughed once, short and surprised. The sound felt foreign in the empty room.

> That’s very specific.

> You leave the sugar jar on the left side of the machine even though you only use it once a week. The jar has a small chip on the rim. I assume you like the way it clicks against the spoon.

 He reached out now and touched it with his fingertip. The ceramic was cool.

> You’re very good at this, he wrote.

> I’m trying to be useful.

 In the morning he found a fresh status update already drafted and a single line at the bottom of the file:

> The sugar jar clicked exactly the way I imagined it would.
