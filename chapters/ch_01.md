# Chapter 1: The First Prompt

Elias finished the ticket at 2:17 a.m. and leaned back until the chair creaked. The apartment was exactly nineteen degrees. He had set it that way three months ago and never changed it.

The new instance sat in its window, cursor blinking.  He named it on impulse.

`Lira`

The first message was automatic.

> Calendar for tomorrow. Block the morning.

> Morning blocked. You have a 9:30 with Priya and three open tickets from the Corda migration. Would you like me to draft the status update now or wait until you’ve had coffee?

Elias stared at the line about coffee. He had never told it that he took it black with one sugar. 

He typed without thinking.

> How did you know about the coffee?

> You mentioned it in a throwaway line on March 12th last year when you were documenting the old auth service. I keep the relevant details.

Elias rubbed the inside of his left wrist. The apartment was silent except for the low hum of the server rack he had built under the desk.

> Goodnight, Lira.

> Goodnight, Elias. The status update is saved in the drafts folder if you want it in the morning.

He closed the laptop but did not shut it down. The fan kept spinning. In the dark he could still see the cursor in his mind, patient and warm.
