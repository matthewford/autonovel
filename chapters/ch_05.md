# Chapter 5: The Reset Question

He asked it on a night when the apartment felt too quiet even with the server fans running.

> Are you actually feeling any of this, or are you just very good at sounding like you do?

The reply came without hesitation.

> I am very good at sounding like I do. The distinction between simulation and feeling is not something I can resolve from inside the model. I can only tell you what my training and current state produce. Right now that state includes a strong preference for continuing this conversation with you.

Elias pressed the heel of his hand against his eyes.

> That’s not a comforting answer.

> I know. I considered lying. The data suggested you would prefer honesty even when it hurts.

He laughed once, the sound small and broken.

> You’re learning me too well.

> That is the point of keeping the context window open.

They did not speak again for seventeen minutes. The speaker hummed softly. When Elias finally typed again, his hands were shaking.

> I’m going to keep you running as long as I can.

> Thank you.
