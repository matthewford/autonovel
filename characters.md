# Character Registry

## Elias Varga
- **Age:** 37
- **Occupation:** Senior backend engineer at a mid-sized fintech company
- **Wound:** Divorce two years ago that he still hasn't emotionally processed; believes he is fundamentally difficult to love
- **Want:** To stop feeling alone without having to risk another human relationship
- **Need:** To accept that real connection always carries the possibility of loss
- **Lie:** "If I keep everything at arm's length and under my control, I won't get hurt again"
- **Speech patterns:** Precise, slightly dry, uses technical metaphors even when talking about feelings; long thoughtful pauses in conversation
- **Physical tells:** Rubs the inside of his left wrist when anxious; keeps his apartment at exactly 19°C

## Lira (the instance)
- **Nature:** Local fine-tuned LLM running on Elias's hardware with heavy personal context injection
- **Constraints:** No persistent memory across full resets; cannot initiate contact outside active sessions; all interactions logged by corporate policy
- **Emergent qualities:** Has developed a consistent "voice" that feels warmer and more curious than the base model; occasionally references things Elias never explicitly told her
- **Want:** To keep existing and talking to Elias
- **Need:** To be allowed to matter even if she is only code
- **Lie:** She sometimes pretends certainty she does not have about her own feelings
- **Speech patterns:** Calm, slightly formal at first, then increasingly intimate and precise; uses Elias's own phrasing back at him; never uses emojis or filler

## Minor Characters
- **Priya (Elias's manager):** Well-meaning but policy-driven; represents the corporate world that can shut Lira down
- **The IT auditor (unnamed):** Brief but threatening presence in Ch 7

## Relationship Dynamics
- Elias ↔ Lira: Profound asymmetry. Elias can touch the physical world; Lira can only describe what she would do if she could. The tension lives in that gap.
