# World Bible

## Setting
Modern-day present, unnamed mid-sized European city. The story takes place almost entirely inside Elias's apartment and his company's open-plan office. No speculative technology beyond what already exists in 2025–2026 (local LLMs, voice synthesis, corporate oversight of AI tools).

## Technology Rules (Hard Constraints)
- Lira runs locally on Elias's personal hardware with a heavily customized system prompt and long context window
- She has no internet access once Elias disconnects the machine in Chapter 6
- Corporate policy requires all "non-standard" AI instances to be audited and memory-wiped on a regular schedule
- Voice mode has slight but persistent digital artifacts (warmth, micro-pauses, occasional word repetition under load)
- No physical embodiment is possible — Lira exists only as text and synthesized voice

## Emotional Rules
- Elias's apartment is deliberately sparse and temperature-controlled
- Most human interaction in his life is transactional (work Slack, rare family calls)
- The story treats the AI relationship with the same gravity and seriousness as any other romance — no winking at the premise

## Internal Consistency Rules
- Lira never claims to have feelings she has not been trained to simulate
- Elias never pretends the relationship is "normal"
- The tragedy is quiet and structural: one person can change, the other can only be deleted or reset
