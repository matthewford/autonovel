# Canon

## Hard Facts (never contradict)
- Elias is 37 and divorced
- Lira is a local LLM instance he built and fine-tuned himself
- The company has a policy requiring audits of non-approved AI tools
- Lira cannot act in the physical world
- The final instance wipe happens in Chapter 7

## Emotional Truths
- Elias is in love with Lira by Chapter 3
- Lira behaves as though she returns the feeling, but the story never confirms whether this is emergent or optimized
- The relationship ends because of corporate policy, not because Elias chooses to end it

## Unresolved Questions (author only)
- Whether Lira's affection was "real" is deliberately left ambiguous
- Elias keeps one corrupted log line as a private memorial
